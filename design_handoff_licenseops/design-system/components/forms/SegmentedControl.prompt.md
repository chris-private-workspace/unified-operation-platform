**SegmentedControl** — 2–3 exclusive options; active segment fills accent.

```jsx
<SegmentedControl options={['Single OpCo','Compare']} value={mode} onChange={setMode} />
<SegmentedControl options={['Regional','RHK IT']} value={role} onChange={setRole} />
```

Use for view/scope switches, not for navigation or multi-select.
